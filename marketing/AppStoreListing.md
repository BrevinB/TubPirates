## Description (4000 char limit — NOT search-indexed on iOS; write for conversion)
```
Captain Pugbeard the pirate pug has seized the bathtub — and he's daring YOU to take it back!

Tub Pirates is a fast, adorable naval strategy game set in a sudsy bathtub arena. Hide your fleet of bath toys in the bubbles, take turns firing cannonballs into your rival's water, and sink all five of their toys before they sink yours.

⚓ OUTSMART A LADDER OF PIRATE CAPTAINS
Duel your way up a rogues' gallery of the tub's most notorious rivals — scruffy Captain Pugbeard, sly Soapy Sal, eight-armed Barnacle Bess, and the decorated final boss himself, Admiral Bubbles. Every captain fights smarter, talks bigger, and pays better loot.

💥 UNLEASH SPECIAL CANNONS
The Parrot Scout spies a whole area of enemy water. The Big Shot smashes four tiles at once. Chain Shot rakes a line, the Flare exposes a hidden ship, and the Fireworks Cannon blasts a five-shot X across the tub. Stock your arsenal in the Armory and fire smart — every special is spent when you use it.

🏴‍☠️ PLUNDER DOUBLOONS
Win battles, claim your daily treasure chest, and double your loot with the first victory of every day. Spend it all in the Armory and the Shipyard.

🦆 COLLECT FLEETS & CAPTAINS
Sail the Ducky Squadron — from a duckling in a walnut shell to an armored duck flagship — or the Sea Monster Crew with its kraken flagship. Collect a crew of avatars from Sailor Pup to the legendary Diamond Quack.

🎮 THREE WAYS TO PLAY
• Battle the captains — a full single-player ladder
• Pass & Play — duel a friend on one device, with a privacy handoff between turns
• Online Battle — turn-based multiplayer through Game Center

🛁 MADE FOR THE TUB
A hand-crafted toy world: circular bathtub arena, suds that hide your fleet, a cannon that actually fires your shots, cartoon sound effects, and a sea-shanty soundtrack.

Easy to learn, quick to play, hard to put down. The water's warm — climb in, Captain.
