# Tub Pirates — Shipaton Build-in-Public Pack

## Strategy in one paragraph
Post 3–5×/week on X (and mirror the best ones to Discord). Every post gets ONE
visual — screenshot, before/after pair, or a 15–30s screen recording. Rotate
four content pillars so the feed doesn't feel samey: (1) juice/polish
before-afters, (2) design decisions with reasoning, (3) monetization/economy
craft (HAMM-award bait — this is RevenueCat's home turf), (4) honest
struggle/fix stories. Always tag #RevenueCatShipaton and @RevenueCat. End
posts with a question when you genuinely want replies — engagement begets
reach.

## The angles only Tub Pirates has
- Childhood nostalgia hook: "rebuilding the Webkinz bathtub battleship game I
  loved as a kid" — leads every intro.
- A four-captain ladder where BOSSES advertise the cosmetics (Bess sails the
  kraken fleet you can buy). Judges love mechanics that double as monetization.
- Earned-only trophies (Gilded Armada) — "the best fleet in the game cannot be
  bought" is a spicy, principled monetization take. Great discussion post.
- Taste-then-scarcity tutorial (full arsenal in a rigged first battle, then
  they're gone). Textbook onboarding-monetization post.
- The art pipeline: AI art + chroma-key script + style-reference chaining.
  Screenshots of magenta-background raw ships next to in-game results.
- Menu that changes as you climb (rival peeking over the tub rim → conquered
  tub). Perfect 4-image carousel.

## Cadence for a Shipaton month
- Wk 1: intro thread + 2 polish posts + Discord intro
- Wk 2: economy design thread + RevenueCat integration post + 1 before/after
- Wk 3: trophy/ladder posts + beta invite (TestFlight link!) + ask-for-feedback
- Wk 4: launch countdown, launch post, numbers/retro thread
- Always: reply to every comment; spend 15 min/day commenting on OTHER
  shipaton builds (this is how the community finds you).

## Rules of thumb
- One visual per post, no exceptions. Recordings > screenshots > text.
- Numbers beat adjectives ("40 lines" > "small change").
- Ship stories, not changelogs: what you decided and WHY.
- Post the failures too (the frog-crab hybrid the AI drew first is a great
  laugh post) — flops humanize the wins.
- Check the current Shipaton rules page for required tags/categories and any
  submission-form specifics — awards and hashtags shift year to year.
