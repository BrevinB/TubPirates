Tub Pirates is a bubbly battleship duel where toy fleets, pirate captains, and special cannon shots turn every bath-time battle into a quick tactical showdown.

Place your fleet, take turns calling shots, and sink every enemy ship before your own boats disappear beneath the bubbles. Play solo against a ladder of mischievous captains, pass one device between two players, or battle online with Game Center.

Features:

- Classic battleship strategy with a playful bathtub pirate theme
- Special weapons like parrots, flares, big shots, chain shots, and fireworks
- Unlockable rival captains with different personalities and rewards
- Collectible fleets, from toy boats to legendary armadas
- Local pass-and-play matches for two players
- Online Game Center battles with quick chat and avatars
- Short sessions built for fast, satisfying duels

Every victory earns doubloons, unlocks new rivals, and helps you build a more ridiculous fleet. Choose your captain, load the cannons, and rule the tub.
